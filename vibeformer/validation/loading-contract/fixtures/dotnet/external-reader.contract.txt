The package-reference-only .NET consumer must launch an isolated configured
external reader process, load real Pkl modules through that process, resolve an
absolute custom-scheme import, and read a text resource. The opaque custom URIs
match the upstream external-reader contract and leave hierarchical listing and
globbing to the separately exercised local, module-path, assembly, embedded,
and package readers. The reader process uses only the packaged public loading
and settings API on its consumer side; its protocol fixture is validation
infrastructure and is not a shipped MessagePack product surface.

The failure/lifecycle probe must run inside a separate package-reference-only
parent so an unhandled receive-thread exception is observed as a child crash,
not as a successful in-process assertion. Across two repetitions it exercises
EOF before initialization, EOF and truncation during module reads, malformed
frames, an unknown response ID, a reader blocked until evaluator cancellation,
and disposal racing a blocked read. Every pending evaluation must finish with a
normal Pkl failure before its bound, every owned process tree must terminate,
the receive thread must quiesce, and a second pass must not grow the parent's
process-handle count. This remains validation of the private configured-reader
framing boundary and does not expose general MessagePack product support.
